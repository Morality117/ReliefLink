Frequently asked questions

Quick answers to common questions.

Does ReliefLink replace emergency services?

No. If your life is in immediate danger, call 112 or the relevant emergency number first. ReliefLink helps coordinate extra support from volunteers.

How do I request help?

Create an account as someone who needs help, then press "Request Help" on your dashboard. Choose what you need, add your location, and send it.

How do I become a volunteer?

Volunteer accounts are created by an admin. Once you have one, log in to see open requests and accept the ones you can take.

Who can see my location?

Your location is attached to your request so volunteers and coordinators can find you. Only share it when you are asking for help.

Can I chat with volunteers?

Yes. When a volunteer is assigned to or accepts your request, a real-time chat opens where you can message each other and share locations.

Does it cost anything?

No. ReliefLink is completely free to use for both citizens requesting assistance and volunteers coordinating disaster relief.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Information We Collect: When you create an account or submit a help request on ReliefLink, we collect your name, email address, phone number, optional profile photo, request details, and the location coordinates you choose to share.

How We Use Information: Your request details and location are shared only with authorized coordinators and assigned volunteers to deliver assistance and enable real-time chat via Nexconn.

Data Storage & Deletion: Account and request records are stored securely using Supabase. You can permanently delete your account and personal request data at any time from your Citizen or Volunteer dashboard.

Contact: For privacy questions, email relieflink.support@gmail.com.

Terms of Service

Last updated: November 2026

Emergency Disclaimer: ReliefLink is a community volunteer-coordination platform and does not replace official emergency services. In immediate danger, always call 112, 108, 101, or 100 first.

Acceptable Use: Users agree to provide accurate location and request details, refrain from submitting false or abusive requests, and communicate respectfully with volunteers and citizens.

Volunteer Coordination: Volunteers act in good faith to assist nearby citizens. ReliefLink and its coordinators do not guarantee response times or availability.

Contact: Questions about these terms can be sent to relieflink.support@gmail.com.
