Frequently asked questions

Quick answers to common questions.

Does ReliefLink replace emergency services?

No. If your life is in immediate danger, call 112 or the relevant emergency number first. ReliefLink helps coordinate extra support from volunteers.

How do I request help?

Create an account as someone who needs help, then press "Request Help" on your dashboard. Choose what you need, add your location, and send it.

How do I become a volunteer?

Sign up and choose "I want to help". You'll see open requests, and you can accept the ones you are able to take.

Who can see my location?

Your location is attached to your request so volunteers can find you. Only share it when you are asking for help.

Can I chat with volunteers?

Yes. When a volunteer accepts your request, a chat opens where you can message each other and share locations.
