Medical Request Verification Policy

To prevent misuse, ReliefLink only provides emergency first aid or short-term (1–3 day) essential chronic medication during disasters. Bulk or non-emergency free medicine requests will be cancelled by an admin, and prescription medicines require showing a valid prescription to the responder.
